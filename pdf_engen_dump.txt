SPECIFICATION FOR TEMPORARY HOARDING & BARRICADING AT RETAIL SITES 3.1 - HOARDING AND BARRICADE TYPES:  The hoarding is to be a minimum 1,8 metres high with a maximum 50mm gap and may be in the form of wire mesh fencing, corrugated iron, structurally supported shade cloth, solid steel interlocking panels, steel fencing, plywood or suitable alternatives.  Note: Orange road cones, hazard tape or flashing lights are considered a form of signage and do not constitute barricading.
